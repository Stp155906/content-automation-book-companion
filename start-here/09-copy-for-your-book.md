# Copy-Ready Book Language

You may copy this wording into your book, sales page, or freebie and customize
the bracketed terms.

## The core promise

> **Never Post Again** does not mean giving away control. It means building a
> content system that handles the repetitive work: creating a predictable
> folder, organizing your images and caption, checking that today's content is
> complete, and preparing it for delivery. You still make the final creative
> decision.

## TikTok draft-first language

> Your content is not automatically made public. The workflow sends a draft to
> the TikTok account you authorized. Open TikTok's inbox notification, make
> any final edits, choose music if you want, and tap Post when you are ready.

## What the reader receives

> This companion includes templates, folder examples, a CSV starter file, and
> a complete draft-first Apps Script reference. Replace the clearly labeled
> placeholders with your own folder IDs and credentials. Never share your
> private tokens or secrets.

## The DIY versus done-for-you choice

> You can follow the DIY steps in this book and customize the system for your
> own workflow. If you prefer not to touch code, [YOUR PRODUCT NAME] may offer
> a separate managed option when available. Platform availability, account
> eligibility, and automation features can change, so always follow each
> platform's current rules.
